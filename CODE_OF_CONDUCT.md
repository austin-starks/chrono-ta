# Code of conduct

Be direct, specific, and respectful. Critique code, methods, and evidence rather
than people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, and
publishing another person's private information are not accepted here.

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these
rules and may restrict participation when necessary. If a conduct report should
not be public, use the private reporting link in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and
put `Conduct report` in the title.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
project-related communication elsewhere.
